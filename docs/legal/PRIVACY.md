# Outbreak Commander — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-10**

## What we collect

- **Steam account identity** (SteamID) when you use online features. We never see your password.
- **Cloud saves** stored via Steam Remote Storage (simulation snapshots + command logs).
- **Leaderboard scores** (days survived, zombies neutralized, outcome).
- **Optional crash reports** (call stacks, GPU/driver, last breadcrumbs). Crash reports are opt-in the first time the game launches after a crash.
- **Optional gameplay telemetry** (win/loss, day reached, adaptive-difficulty bias). Off by default; toggle in Settings → Privacy.

We do **not** collect: microphone audio, camera, contacts, precise GPS, or payment details (Steam handles payments).

## Why

Operate multiplayer, cloud saves, leaderboards, crash triage, and (if you opt in) balance telemetry. Legal bases: contract (to provide the Game) and legitimate interest (stability). Opt-in telemetry uses consent.

## Sharing

- **Valve / Steam** — identity, cloud, leaderboards, Workshop.
- **Crash backend** (Sentry, if a DSN is configured at packaging) — crash dumps you opted to send.
- We do not sell personal data.

## Retention

Cloud saves live until you delete them or your Steam account. Crash reports 90 days. Telemetry 13 months. Leaderboards for the life of the product.

## Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, export, and objection as provided by GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA. Contact privacy@outbreakcommander.example. You can also delete cloud saves from the in-game Save menu.

## Children

The Game is rated for teens (see IARC). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes

Material changes will be posted in-game and on the Steam store page.
