# Outbreak Commander — Steam store copy

## Short description (300 chars)

Command the city, not the trigger. A god-view city-management and zombie-survival game: raise walls, keep the lights on, research a vaccine, and hold the horde. Co-op 2–8, versus, and a 30-minute story mission.

## Long description

**You don't fight every zombie. You command the city.**

Outbreak Commander is a god-view city-management game about containing a virus. Infection spreads through bites, contaminated ground, panic, and infrastructure failure. You build, tax, research, and order squads — never click-to-stab.

### Command

- Drag-to-build walls with live preview, auto-corners, and gates
- Twelve building types with upgrades, power and water networks, day/night solar
- A full economy: money, food, water, fuel, materials, medicine, ammo, research
- Emergency policies: curfew, quarantine, martial law
- International aid, trade caravans, evacuation flights

### Survive

- Five zombie types with real AI: pack cohesion, target memory, noise attraction, wall-slide flanking
- Military: militia to spec ops, trucks to helicopters, airstrikes, and the nuclear option (the world will not forgive you)
- Tech tree (15 techs, 5 branches) ending in the vaccine
- Win by eradication, containment, or evacuation — or lose when the city is overwhelmed

### Replay

- Campaign (tutorial + story missions), endless sandbox, custom rules, seeds
- Co-op 2–8 with roles (Commander, Engineer, Medic, Logistics)
- Versus: one player commands the virus
- Deterministic simulation: the same seed and commands replay the same outbreak
- After-action reports, Steam achievements, cloud saves, leaderboards

### Accessibility

Full controller / Steam Input, colorblind modes, subtitles, text and UI scale, reduced motion. Languages: English, German, Persian, Arabic.

Built in Unreal Engine 5 with Lumen and Nanite. Verified target: 60 FPS at 1080p High on RTX 3060-class hardware; Steam Deck at Medium + FSR.
